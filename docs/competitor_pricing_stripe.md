# Competitive Analysis: Stripe Pricing

**Source**: https://www.stripe.com/pricing  
**Scraped Date**: October 13, 2025

## Key Pricing Insights

### Standard Pricing
- **Online Payments**: 2.9% + 30¢ per successful transaction for domestic cards
- **In-Person Payments**: 2.7% + 5¢ per successful transaction for domestic cards
- **International Cards**: +1.5% additional fee
- **Currency Conversion**: +1% if required

### Payment Methods
- **Link (Accelerated Checkout)**: 2.9% + 30¢
- **Instant Bank Payments**: 2.6% + 30¢
- **Klarna (BNPL)**: 5.99% + 30¢
- **ACH Direct Debit**: 0.8% ($5.00 cap)
- **Stablecoin Payments**: 1.5% of transaction amount

### Additional Services
- **Radar (Fraud Detection)**: Included for standard pricing, 5¢ per transaction for custom pricing
- **Radar for Fraud Teams**: 2¢ per transaction (standard), 7¢ (custom)
- **Disputes**: $15.00 per dispute received
- **Instant Payouts**: 1.5% of volume (50¢ minimum)
- **Billing**: Starting at $620/month or 0.7% of volume
- **Tax**: Starting at $90/month or 0.5% per transaction
- **Invoicing**: 0.4% per paid invoice

### Terminal (In-Person)
- **Card Readers**: $59 (M2) to $349 (S700)
- **Tap to Pay**: +10¢ per authorization

### Connect (Platforms)
- **Revenue Share**: 0.25% starting fee for custom pricing models

### Financial Services
- **Financial Accounts**: Included, no storage fees
- **Wire Funding**: $2.00 per wire
- **Domestic Payouts**: $1.50 per payout
- **International Payouts**: $1.50 + 0.25% cross-border + 0.5% FX fees

### Issuing
- **Virtual Cards**: 10¢ per card
- **Physical Cards**: $3.50 per standard card
- **Cross-Border**: 1% + 30¢ + 1% FX conversion

## Competitive Positioning for Our SaaS Platform

Based on this analysis, our platform should:
1. Offer competitive pricing at 2.5% + 25¢ for standard transactions
2. Include basic fraud protection at no additional cost
3. Provide free invoicing for the first 100 invoices/month
4. Bundle subscription management with no monthly fee for startups
5. Offer transparent pricing with no hidden fees
